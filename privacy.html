Privacy

Sahiti’s Planner is a personal planner used only by its owner, Sahiti Sarma.

What it accesses

When connected, the planner reads and updates Google Calendar events and Google Tasks, and stores planner data in a private app-data folder in the owner’s own Google Drive. This is only used to show and sync the owner’s own planner.

Where data is kept

Planner content is saved on the owner’s devices and in the owner’s own Google account. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. A small helper server keeps the Google connection active; it stores only a sign-in token, not calendar or planner content.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the planner’s Settings (Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
